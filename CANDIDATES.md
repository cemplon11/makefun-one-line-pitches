# Candidate one-line pitches

Task: one sentence, at most 240 characters, that would convince a friend who has
never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun.
Judge criteria, in order: persuasion, clarity, truth, warmth and craft.

## Candidates

1. Tell me your app idea in plain words and I will have an AI agent build it for you on make.fun, no coding needed and free to try.
2. You know that app idea you have been sitting on? Just describe it in plain words and an AI agent on make.fun will build it for you, no coding needed.
3. If you can describe it, you can have it: tell an AI agent on make.fun what you want and watch your idea become a real app, no coding required.
4. Your idea does not need code, it needs a description: tell an AI agent on make.fun what you want and it builds the app for you, free to try.
5. Give your idea a voice and let an AI agent do the building: describe the app you want on make.fun and it becomes real, even if you have never coded.
6. You do not have to learn to code, just tell an AI agent on make.fun what app you dream of and it will build it for you.
7. That app idea in your head? Say it out loud on make.fun and an AI agent turns it into a working app, no coding, no experience, just your idea.
8. Stop waiting to learn code: describe the app you want to an AI agent on make.fun and it will build it for you, free to try.
9. Your first app is one sentence away: tell an AI agent on make.fun your idea in plain words and it builds it, no coding required.
10. Imagine telling a friend your app idea and having it built by morning, that is what an AI agent on make.fun does, and you do not need to code a thing.

## Pick

Refined from candidate 1 (fixing the ambiguous "tell me" — the friend tells the
agent, not the speaker):

> Tell an AI agent on make.fun your app idea in plain words and it will build the app for you, no coding needed, free to try.

123 characters. It is direct and second-person: it names the friend's idea, the
exact action (tell the agent in plain words), the mechanism (an AI agent builds
it), the place (make.fun), and two low-risk hooks (no coding, free to try). It
is plain English with no jargon, fully truthful (make.fun entries are free to
try and the agent does the building), and it reads like a person talking to a
friend rather than a slogan. It lands in one read.
